# Assets & intellectual-property policy

This project is a fan-made, non-commercial, open-source save editor for
Borderlands 2. To keep the repository clean to publish and safe for others to
fork, we follow one rule:

> **We do not bundle, redistribute, or hotlink any Gearbox/2K copyrighted or
> trademarked assets** — no game icons, character art, the vault symbol, the
> Borderlands logo, fonts, or extracted art files.

"Non-commercial" and "open-source" do **not** grant a licence to redistribute
someone else's copyrighted work; this rule keeps us on the safe side regardless.

## What we DO instead

- **Original art only.** The GUI's look (`bl2-gui/src/theme.rs`) is our own:
  a warm near-black + amber palette and hand-drawn shapes (a hexagon emblem,
  a coin, an eridium gem). It *evokes* the cel-shaded style — which is not
  copyrightable — without copying any specific artwork.
- **Nominative naming.** We may call this "a save editor for Borderlands 2" to
  describe compatibility. We do **not** use the official stylised logo or imply
  endorsement by Gearbox/2K.
- **We stick to original art.** We are not extracting or bundling game icons —
  the original themes are enough. (If real in-game icons were ever wanted, the only
  acceptable route would be reading them from the user's *own* installed copy at
  runtime — never committing them here — but we are not pursuing that.)

## For contributors

Please do not add official game art, logos, fonts, or extracted assets to the
repo. Original art or assets under a permissive/public-domain licence (with the
licence noted) are welcome.

Borderlands, the Borderlands logo, and related marks are trademarks of Gearbox
Software / 2K. This project is unaffiliated and unofficial.
